# 03 — Domain Modules (High-Level)

**Read this first:** this file describes what each module is for and its key concepts. It is **not an implementation spec**. Before starting a phase, the agent writes a detailed spec (entities, columns, endpoints, permissions, audit events, tests, UI screens), lists the open questions, and waits for the owner's approval (see `01-roadmap.md`). Do not implement ahead of the roadmap.

All modules obey the rules in `.agents/rules/`. In particular: permission-based access, audit on every clinical write and patient-data read, no hard deletes, versioned/append-only clinical data, and exported-service-only cross-module access.

## Reception and Registration (Phase 1)

- **Patient**: system UUID plus human-friendly MRN, demographics (names, sex, date of birth or estimated age, contact details, address), next of kin/emergency contact, identification documents (stored carefully; never fully logged), consent flags, status.
- **MRN**: generated by the database, unique, human-friendly, configurable prefix; format finalized in the Phase 1 spec.
- **Search and duplicates**: fast search by name, MRN, phone, date of birth using trigram indexes; duplicate candidates shown at registration; merge is linking, reversible, audited.
- **Patient card**: printable card with name, MRN, and barcode/QR; reprint is audited.
- **Visit/Encounter**: opened at reception, has type (outpatient, emergency, follow-up), department, status flow, queue token. Visits are the parent of consultations, orders, and charges.
- **Queue**: per-department waiting list and token calling.

## Clinical: Nursing and Doctor (Phase 2)

- **Departments and staff profiles**: doctors' specialties and license info, departments hospitals may extend (lookup tables).
- **Triage/vitals**: structured observations with units (temperature, pulse, blood pressure, respiratory rate, SpO2, weight, height), each with `occurred_at` and `recorded_at`.
- **Consultation**: chief complaint, history, examination, assessment, diagnoses (ICD-10 coded), plan. Stored as revisions; corrections keep history.
- **Orders**: lab, imaging, procedure orders linked to the visit; status flow.
- **Prescriptions**: structured medication requests (drug, dose amount and unit, route, frequency, duration, quantity, instructions); statuses (active, partially dispensed, dispensed, cancelled).
- **Patient timeline**: chronological view of visits, vitals, diagnoses, results, prescriptions.
- **Referrals and follow-ups**: appointments and referral records.

## Pharmacy (Phase 3)

- **Drug catalog**: generic and brand names, ATC code, dosage form, strength, unit, active/inactive.
- **Stock**: batches with lot number, expiry, quantity; movements ledger (receipt, dispense, return, adjustment, write-off); balances derived from movements; never negative.
- **Suppliers and receiving**: goods received notes.
- **Dispensing**: works from the prescription queue; verifies patient, prescription status, and quantity; FEFO batch selection; atomic and idempotent; partial dispensing supported.
- **Safety checks**: allergy and interaction checks (data source decided with the owner; "check not performed" is shown if unavailable); expired batches blocked.
- **Controlled substances**: separate register and stricter permissions.
- **Alerts**: low stock, near-expiry.

## Laboratory (Phase 4)

- **Test catalog**: tests, panels, specimen types, units, reference ranges (LOINC-compatible fields).
- **Orders and samples**: order queue, sample collection and tracking, rejection reasons.
- **Results**: structured values with units and flags computed from approved ranges; entry, validation, and corrections as superseding revisions.
- **Reports**: printable lab report; results visible to the ordering doctor.

## Billing (Phase 5)

- **Price lists and services**: consultation fees, procedures, lab tests, drugs; effective dating.
- **Charges**: captured automatically from visits, orders, and dispensing.
- **Invoices and payments**: exact-decimal amounts; issued invoices are immutable; payments and refunds are ledger entries; receipts printable.
- **Credit/insurance**: model decided with the owner.
- **Cashier shifts**: opening, closing, reconciliation, daily cash report.

## Inpatient (Phase 6)

- **Wards, rooms, beds**: bed status and occupancy.
- **Admission, transfer, discharge**: linked to visits; bed history retained.
- **Nursing charting and MAR**: scheduled medications, administration records with who/when.
- **Discharge summary**: structured summary printable for the patient.

## Supporting modules (Phase 7)

- Imaging/radiology reports, general inventory and procurement, staff scheduling and shifts, reports and analytics (daily visits, revenue, disease trends, stock).

## Cross-cutting (all phases)

- **Permissions**: each module adds its permission codes to the catalog in `packages/shared` and to its seed for the default roles.
- **Audit**: each module's spec lists its audit events.
- **Printing**: patient card, prescriptions, receipts, lab reports use print-optimized views.
- **Search**: `pg_trgm`-based; no external search engine.
- **Reporting**: read-only queries; heavy reports must not slow clinical workflows (materialized views or scheduled aggregation if needed, decided per spec).
